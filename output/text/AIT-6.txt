Coverage Summary:
Covers core journey-type support (Round Trip default, One-Way, Multi-City), mandatory airport validations, origin/destination non-equality validation, airport search inputs (code/name/city) with dynamic suggestions, popular destinations display (no input), retention of selected values, and a proxy check for responsive behavior via viewport-based smoke coverage. Includes negative and boundary scenarios for key validation risks. Gaps remain where configuration/CMS-driven behavior and accessibility standards are not objectively verifiable from requirements/UI without additional details.
